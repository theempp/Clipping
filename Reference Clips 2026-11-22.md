## What they share
- **One topic everyone is watching:** every clip is GTA 6 with a roster streamer (fits D069/D070). Timeliness is the hook.
- **Short:** 12-31 s for 6 of 7. Only the story clip runs 77 s. The payoff lands within 20 s.
- **The headline caption carries the clip.** One static caption sits above the video for the whole clip, in TikTok's native box style (white box with black text, or a red box for a quote). It names the person, the claim, a CAPS stress word (OFFICIALLY, FIRST, LOSES IT, STILL) and ends on 1-2 emoji (😭😂👀🥹). None of these use word-by-word captions.
- **Face plus gameplay:** most stack the streamer's face cam above the gameplay, or cut back and forth between them. The reaction face is the emotional payoff.
- **Punchline edits:** the game's own WASTED screen, a slow push-in, a black-and-white switch for drama, or a short meme reaction insert at the end.
- **Emotions to aim for:** hype/triumph, rage, disbelief, a relatable self-own, slapstick fail, wholesome-then-funny. Big face reactions beat quiet gameplay.

## What this means for Clipp (proposals, not decisions)
1. Add a **headline caption** (static title box, CAPS stress word, emoji) as the default top text. The judge already writes titles; ask it for this format. Keep word captions (D048) optional underneath or off.
2. Default layout **face cam stacked over gameplay** when a crop box exists (roster crop boxes already do this).
3. Target 12-30 s and cut so the reaction lands in the last third. Allow up to ~80 s only for a story arc.
4. Detector/judge should favour moments with a strong face reaction (shout, hands on face, dance), not just chat spikes.
5. Optional punchline effects later: B&W switch, slow push-in on the reaction.

The TikTok end card and watermark in these clips are from re-downloading; ours must not copy other accounts' marks.
