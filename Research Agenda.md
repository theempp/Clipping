## Evidence standard
Date findings. Prefer official platform/API documentation and attributable audience statistics. Separate demonstrated facts, creator claims, estimates, hypotheses and user decisions. State unavailable metrics. Avoid invented revenue projections and universal claims about best clip lengths.

## Research tracks
1. Eight-streamer roster: audience, trend, overlap, clip demand, source access and permissions.
2. Platform distribution: retention, audience fit, publishing/analytics access, account requirements and monetization rules.
3. Editing: hooks, context, subtitles, reframing, clip duration and content category. Test recommendations against results.
4. Technical feasibility: ingest, embeds, Grok, transcription, editing, publishing, analytics and operating costs.
5. Learning: feedback schema, experiments, measurement windows and auditability.
6. Monetization: revenue model, eligibility, originality requirements, cost per approved clip and sustainable output.
7. Launch strategy: narrow enough to operate reliably while supporting broader reach.
