## User direction - 2026-11-20
V1 revenue should prioritize creator campaigns through Content Rewards. Zo requests a future agent or skill that repeatedly checks campaigns associated with top streamers, studies which to pursue or avoid, and explains why so Zo learns alongside the system.

Recommendations should improve with observed editing strengths, streamer familiarity, channel performance and other relevant outcomes. This is a planning requirement, not a deployed monitor or learning service. Check frequency, access method, launch scope and adaptation authority remain open. No campaign, roster change, enrollment or publication is approved by this direction alone.

## Proposed decision process - not yet approved
1. Discover campaigns through supported access; store URL, identity, brief, observation time and change history. Distinguish inactive, inaccessible and unknown status. No authenticated access or API has been validated.
2. Check source permissions, account and platform eligibility, required disclosures, approved assets, submission deadlines and campaign availability before scoring fit. Missing evidence means investigate, not eligible.
3. Compare likely net earnings against production cost, review time and opportunity cost. Track payout model, fees, minimums, caps, available funding, deadlines and payment uncertainty. Advertised rates alone do not establish profitability.
4. Assess streamer familiarity, content style, editing capabilities, audience match and destination performance. Separate observed performance from hypotheses; do not let one viral result dominate.
5. Present pursue / small test / avoid / insufficient evidence, with reasons, sources, uncertainty, alternatives and next action. Labels and scoring weights are proposals.
6. Preserve Zo's feedback, actual approved/rejected submissions, verified views, production costs and paid outcomes. Keep estimated, pending, settled and withdrawn earnings distinct. Use comparable cohorts and explicit experiments; proposals must be reversible and auditable.

## Proposed acceptance criteria for eventual handoff
- Each recommendation includes a dated campaign source, reasons, uncertainty and eligibility gaps.
- Unknown campaign data cannot silently become zero or a verified fact.
- Rule changes trigger re-evaluation of affected queued work; retain the old brief and reasoning.
- Zo's publishing approval remains required. Campaign-side draft and payment reviews are separate states.
- Track publication time, submission deadline and confirmed submission receipt; surface failure before the deadline. A manual fallback must be available if supported automation cannot be validated.
- No claim of learning without recorded feedback/outcomes and a versioned recommendation change.
- No automatic roster, channel or publishing-authority change is implied by a recommendation.

## Initial official research - accessed 2026-11-20
Sources: https://contentrewards.com/faqs and https://contentrewards.com/terms (terms updated November 17, 2026).

Official documentation lists CPM, per-post, retainer and pool models. The terms require adulthood, campaign compliance and relevant rights. Current rules require submission within 30 minutes of publication. Approval and funding affect payment; pool estimates are uncertain. CPM/pool creator fees are 10%; other fees depend on campaign type/budget. Some campaigns require draft review. Social connections used by Content Rewards do not grant our tool publishing access. Campaign-specific terms and any applicable legacy terms still need checking.

Implication (our planning inference): evaluate opportunities using net outcomes and fit, preserve campaign rule snapshots, and plan a separate submission workflow. No active campaign for any proposed streamer has been verified, and no earnings or account eligibility is established.

## Open research
- Supported discovery, polling limits, notifications, submissions and analytics/export access; no public API assumed.
- Identity and legitimacy of each campaign organizer; streamer authorization and permissible source footage.
- Evidence of paid outcomes versus promotional earnings claims.
- Country/account requirements, platform paid-content rules and actual account readiness.
- Freshness checks, budget depletion, rule changes, monitoring cadence and review workload.
- Cold-start recommendations before our own channels have enough comparable results.
