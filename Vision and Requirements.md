# Vision and Requirements
Links: [[clipping]] � [[Decision Log]] � [[Agent Workflow]] � [[Research Agenda]]

Build a private clipping tool that follows eight selected live streamers, detects promising moments, clips and edits in the background, and presents finished content for human review before publishing.

## User requirements
- Continuous monitoring must continue while clips are extracted, edited or reviewed.
- Eight streamer avatars in navigation; profile photos and a green outline/glow when live.
- Dedicated streamer pages with the actual livestream window as the hero.
- Visible agent activity: watching, detecting, clipping, editing and waiting for review; simultaneous work should be visible.
- Current live analytics and numbers, with clear availability and freshness.
- Generated clips with approve, deny and request-revision actions. Revisions should not require deletion.
- Approved clips publish to their assigned channels.
- Potential destinations: TikTok, YouTube, X, Instagram. Exact V1 coverage is open.
- Research deeply into streamer, platform and content-style performance.
- Simple, minimal, straightforward product experience; detailed research underneath.
- Improve efficiency and results over time.
- Latest goal: reach the broadest practical audience while maximizing retention and monetization.
- Grok bot is proposed. CapCut was offered as a possible editing program, not required or technically validated.
- Codex establishes a detailed plan; Claude Code implements later.

## Constraints requiring definition
Budget, hosting, stream access, concurrency, detection quality, turnaround targets, retention/storage, source permissions, publishing access, account ownership, measurable acceptance criteria.

No guarantee of catching every viral moment. Measure coverage and failures rather than promising perfection.
